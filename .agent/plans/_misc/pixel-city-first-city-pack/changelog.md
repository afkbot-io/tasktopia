# Changelog

## Added

- 12 modular terrain/surface/overlay tiles on the 8px grid.
- 20 building families with five construction stages each.
- Four exact car palette variants in two orientations.
- Runtime atlas, manifest, catalogs, city proof and AI placement documentation.

## Changed

- Crosswalks and bridge details are explicit 8×8 overlays.
- Building placement uses independent canvas, footprint and exact bottom-center anchor.

## Removed

- Runtime-drawn road markings and crosswalks from the new V3 scene contract.
